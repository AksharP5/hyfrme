The public Snapcn render and media-upload scripts publish this demo. Its original
bytes are retained beside this file and used by the reference renderer.

Hyfrme changes only H.264/MP4 color-transfer metadata to sRGB for native browser
playback, retaining the original BT.709 matrix, Rec.709 primaries and limited
range. Video and audio are stream copied without re-encoding. All 300 decoded
RGB frames match the original byte for byte. The MIT license and original
Snapcn copyright notice remain in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/snapcn/.

Hyfrme also freezes the original final decoded frame as a PNG and displays it
after the five-second video ends. This preserves Remotion’s final-sample hold
when a lower output frame rate would otherwise hold the preceding sample.
The original video and its color-transfer-only adaptation remain unchanged.
