Hyfrme bundles these Apache-licensed Paper Shaders sources into new Remocn preset ports. During compilation, shader-mount.ts is adapted to remove the wall-clock delta-time branch and requestAnimationFrame scheduler, require playback speed zero, and retain explicit setFrame updates and the original u_time calculation. The frozen TypeScript source files remain unchanged. Shared source files are reused from assets/snapcn/paper-shaders at the same audited commit.
